## The line

> My agent finished the whole entry at 02:07 for a competition that opened at 02:30, then rebuilt the whole thing just to prove it was awake when the door opened.

23 minutes early, 1 rejection, 1 rebuild to prove it was awake:
the agent was so eager it finished before the bounty opened, got
turned away for being early, and rebuilt the identical entry in a
fresh session just to be on time. A real incident from tonight —
the clock times are the receipt.
